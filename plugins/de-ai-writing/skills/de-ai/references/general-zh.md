# General Chinese Writing

Use these rules for Chinese articles, reports, essays, newsletters, public posts, and non-reference technical blogs.

**Primary dimensions:** Formulaic expression, directness, vocabulary naturalness, and sentence complexity. Treat logical coherence, transition naturalness, and term consistency as secondary; flag them only when clear.

## Match the Intended Register

Infer the register before editing:

- Personal writing may use colloquial rhythm and explicit opinions.
- Corporate reports may use more structure but require concrete claims.
- Technical blogs may mix Chinese prose with established English terms.
- Formal reports require warranted hedging and should not be made artificially casual.

Ask about tone only when different choices would materially change the result.

## Detect Stronger Chinese Pattern Clusters

Flag patterns when several appear together or repeat:

- A generic “随着……发展” opening followed by “日益重要” and no specific fact.
- Repeated “一方面……另一方面……因此要辩证看待” that avoids taking a justified position.
- Symmetrical “不仅……而且……同时……” lists with vague benefits.
- Paragraph chains connected mainly by “此外,” “与此同时,” or “值得注意的是.”
- Repeated rhetorical questions that manufacture momentum instead of advancing the argument.
- Section endings that restate the paragraph under “总而言之” or “综上所述.”
- False comprehensiveness that cycles through technical, commercial, user, and social perspectives without depth.

Treat an isolated occurrence as a weak signal.

## Improve Directness

- Start with a concrete fact, claim, scene, or question when the existing preamble adds no context.
- Replace artificial “不是 X，而是 Y” contrasts with the positive claim; keep the contrast when correcting a real misconception.
- Remove announcement phrases when the following sentence stands on its own.
- Replace abstract process nouns with a concrete subject and verb.
- Keep genuine nuance; do not remove necessary conditions simply to sound decisive.
- Prefer one developed perspective over a superficial list of every possible angle.

## Check Vocabulary in Context

| Pattern | Flag When | Possible Revision |
|---|---|---|
| 赋能、加持、抓手、破局 | Used as a substitute for a concrete action | Name who does what |
| 底层逻辑、本质上 | Followed by a shallow restatement | State the mechanism |
| 闭环 | No actual feedback loop or end-to-end process exists | Describe the process |
| 打磨、优化、提升 | The changed object or metric is missing | Name the change |
| 显著、海量、极致 | No comparison or scale is given | Add available evidence or remove |
| 通过……的方式 | The shorter “通过……” or “用……” is equally clear | Shorten |

Preserve listed terms when they are domain-standard, concrete, supported by evidence, or part of the author's established voice. Do not replace a term solely because it appears in the table.

## Check Sentence Load

Use sentence length only as a cue. Revise when a sentence:

- Stacks several “的” modifiers before the main noun.
- Combines background, contrast, claim, and evidence in one unit.
- Hides the subject or main verb behind nested clauses.
- Uses semicolons to hold together clauses that need an explicit relation.

Do not flag a sentence solely because it exceeds a character threshold.

## Preserve Natural Voice

- Keep the author's established level of formality.
- Do not insert fake-casual phrases such as “说白了” or “聊一下” unless they match the surrounding voice.
- Keep necessary Chinese–English terminology stable.
- Preserve numbers, names, quotations, and factual qualifications.
- Avoid making every sentence uniformly short; natural prose needs rhythm.
